# Slides and Presentation-Like Display Mode

Use this reference for slide decks, linear presentation HTML, keynote-style documents, and other artifacts whose primary job is to advance an audience through an argument or story.

## Narrative Contract

For each page or slide, identify:

- the claim or audience takeaway;
- why it appears at this point;
- what it receives from the prior page;
- what it prepares for the next page;
- the evidence or example that earns the claim.

Use a topic title only when a conclusion title would overstate the evidence. Do not require every slide to function as a standalone infographic.

## Visual Direction

- Let the deck's argument create visual rhythm across pages.
- Use repeated composition intentionally for comparison or a recurring chapter structure; otherwise vary scale, density, imagery, and spatial organization according to the narrative.
- Keep unequal information at unequal visual weight.
- Use open canvas where grouping does not require a container.
- Represent lifecycle, scope, and comparison spatially before reaching for numbered cards.

## Concept Images

Offer a GPT Image 2 or approved image-generation pass when original imagery, atmosphere, material, scenario illustration, or a visual metaphor would materially improve audience understanding or memory. Ask for or generate only the assets the selected direction actually needs.

Skip generated imagery when the deck is governed by a strict template, the page must show exact product state or source evidence, or imagery would imply facts that are not supported. Clearly separate illustrative concepts from evidence.

## Verification

Inspect:

- every page at full size;
- a full-deck contact sheet;
- titles alone as an argument outline;
- the spoken transition between adjacent pages;
- representative projection or small-screen distance;
- three-page neighborhoods for accidental template repetition;
- export fidelity, cropping, font substitution, and overflow.

Fix any break in the narrative chain or critical visual defect before completion.
