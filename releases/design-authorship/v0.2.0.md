## Change

- Added one focused product-structure reference: situated task, real content, objects, actions, states, information organization, and proportionate prototype choice.
- Made attention continuity a design and review concern across the whole composition, action feedback, responsive changes, and return to context. Predicted attention remains distinct from observed gaze or user-study evidence.
- Expanded the existing durable-design reference to guide ongoing work even when the project already has a design owner. Retained composition, hierarchy, boundary meanings, role-based styling, flexible details, deliberate variation, and reproducible accepted examples.
- Closed the maintenance cycle through explicit feedback, cause-directed revision, affected and shared-impact verification, and replacement of superseded baseline decisions. Temporary experiments and incidental implementation do not silently become accepted rules; valid baseline decisions do not freeze demonstrated defects.
- Preserved artifact routes, reasonable card collections, approved directions, delegation, and bounded checks. Clarified that a new single-platform product does not need unsupported surfaces; corrected a scenario that confused explicit asset authorization with mere asset access.
- Removed the dated claim that one particular image model is current. Exact tool/model choices remain subject to available capability and current verification when needed.
