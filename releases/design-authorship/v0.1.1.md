## Scope

The user approved change-scoped slide verification during the Astra compatibility review. New decks and whole-deck redesigns retain full narrative, visual, and export review. Local edits check changed pages, relevant neighbors, and dependent content while retaining valid unaffected evidence. Saving, shared styles, or export behavior can broaden integrity checks to every affected page without reopening the approved narrative by default.

The entrypoint and critique reference point to the same scope decision in `references/slides.md`. UI alignment, design delegation, concept-image policy, diagram verification, construction tools, and publication authority remain unchanged.
