# design-authorship v0.1.2

Date: 2026-09-05

Status: validated local candidate

## Change

- Reuse an authorized image tool and explicitly delegated concept selection; ask only about remaining material decisions. Preserve private-asset authorization and the distinction between concept imagery and production evidence.
- Verify affected supported layouts and materially changed states within the confirmed or delegated UI contract. Broaden checks for shared components or styles without inventing an unsupported platform.
- Follow an already-selected design direction. Compare meaningful alternatives when a material direction is unresolved, without requiring a fixed number of directions or differing axes.

The new-UI and material-interaction agreement or delegation requirement remains in place. This version includes the v0.1.1 slide-verification scope refinement.

## Focused Evidence

An independent GPT-6 Astra decision exercise with requested effort `xhigh` used three synthetic situations and current runtime instructions, without receiving expected answers or prior conclusions. It reused existing image-tool and concept-selection delegation, kept a desktop-only kiosk repair on its supported surface, and followed an approved composition in a new project without manufacturing alternatives. All three decisions met the scoped expectations.

No image, UI, or export was produced in this exercise. These results assess the workflow decisions, not rendered quality or long-horizon performance. The three new cases are also retained as evaluation inputs; deterministic input checks do not establish model behavior.
