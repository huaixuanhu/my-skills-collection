## Scope

The user approved two governance changes during the Astra compatibility review, alongside the slide-verification change in [design-authorship v0.1.1](../design-authorship/v0.1.1.md).

- Report graph-rebuild need, authorization, execution, and verification from actual state. Continue a rebuild already covered by the accepted scope, and preserve owner-source authority over the derived graph.
- Preserve the accepted objective across side questions and compatible additions. On explicit cancellation or replacement, stop future incompatible actions, reconcile operations already started, and reuse unaffected work and evidence. A queued instruction or cancellation request does not establish that an operation stopped or was undone.
