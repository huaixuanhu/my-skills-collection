# human-ai-governance v0.7.1

Date: 2026-08-21

Status: validated canonical candidate

## Scope

- Keep engineering-governance writing as the default for operational sources of truth.
- Activate audience-facing expression from the content unit's audience and function rather than file extension, length, or a generic request for polish.
- Route mixed reports and presentations by section, page, slide, note, or appendix.
- Minimize repeated self-justification, process defense, irrelevant boundary narration, and recurring templated rhetoric in reader-facing work.
- Preserve facts, citations, uncertainty, evidence ceilings, required disclosure, safety controls, project tier, authorization, and validation across both writing modes.
- Treat repeated negative parallelism, em dashes, list-heavy structure, and similar patterns as editorial diagnostics rather than forbidden forms.
- Keep the generic preflight behavior unchanged apart from the v0.7.1 marker; do not add phrase, punctuation, detector, or style-score gates.
