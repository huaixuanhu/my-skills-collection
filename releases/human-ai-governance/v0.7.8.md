## Change

Before implementing an unapproved safeguard that expands the accepted design, the agent must explain the credible failure and evidence, existing protection, added costs, simpler alternatives and their remaining risks, and its recommendation, then wait for the user's choice. Announcing a plan, silence, or a broad robustness request does not approve undisclosed machinery. Related small additions are considered together when they form a larger protection system.

Routine error handling, explicitly required controls, and already-approved safeguards continue inside their existing scope. A declined optional addition stays declined unless relevant evidence or scope changes. Mandatory boundaries remain in force; a newly discovered gap leads to the smallest compliant remedy or scope reduction, with only the affected operation paused.

The rule is placed in the existing entrypoint, proportional-assurance reference, and generated project template. Migration guidance and seven representative scenarios cover both overbuilding and unnecessary permission requests. No safeguard counter, keyword-based approval check, risk score, registry, or new preflight logic is added. The generic preflight script changes only its version marker.
