# human-ai-governance v0.7.4

Date: 2026-08-28

Status: validated canonical candidate

## Scope

- Treat an explicit “continue until verifiable success” request as a persistent completion directive whose objective survives failed attempts.
- Separate the accepted completion objective, safe in-scope recovery, and consequence-bearing execution so a failed live attempt does not consume authority to diagnose, repair, validate, checkpoint, or prepare the next candidate.
- Route another attempt by credible single and cumulative consequences instead of its attempt number.
- Continue equivalent or adaptive recovery while scope, success criteria, authority, accepted state, affected systems, and consequence ceiling remain unchanged.
- After a consequential failure, advance safe recovery to the next real boundary and pause only before an action that needs renewed authority.
- Preserve explicit one-shot gates, project hard limits, platform approvals, accepted artifacts, receipts, checkpoints, rollback routes, and validation rules.
- Support pre-authorized multi-attempt execution envelopes with named targets, limits, time windows, health checks, reconciliation behavior, and stop conditions.
- Reject a universal retry count and require progress after substantive repeated failure rather than an unchanged mechanical loop.

## Compatibility Contract

The five-tier model, reasoning routing, v0.7.1 engineering-governance and audience-facing writing modes, v0.7.2 technical-language choices, and v0.7.3 plan-index routing remain unchanged. Persistent completion affects recovery and authorization routing only. It does not reduce reasoning depth, implementation quality, validation, evidence, safety, or exact technical records.

The directive cannot add deployment, publication, push, live action, new credentials, data movement, spending, or external effects that were outside the accepted scope. Named consequence gates and the platform's current approvals remain authoritative. A plan index may locate the authority owner but cannot grant or renew authority.

## Behavioral Evidence Ceiling

- Added scenarios for persistent local validation failure, a transport-failed authorized non-force push, a production activation no-go, a pre-authorized multi-attempt envelope, an ambiguous non-idempotent external result, and recovery around an accepted artifact.
- Added semantic checks for the three authority layers, consequence-based attempt routing, cumulative limits, safe recovery to the exact boundary, reconciliation, and preservation of accepted state.
- No independent model-output matrix was run for this release. Deterministic checks prove that the skill package carries the intended contract; they do not prove identical behavior in every future repository or model response.
- Earlier writing, terminology, and plan-routing invariants remain applicable because their selection rules and capability boundaries are unchanged.
