# human-ai-governance v0.6.1

Date: 2026-08-10

Status: validated canonical release

## Scope

- Route Max and Ultra by task-topology certainty and the dominant coordination need, not by agent count.
- Prefer Max for stable decomposition whose bounded parallel work must feed one coherent causal or decision chain.
- Prefer Ultra for uncertain or evolving decomposition and for separable parallel work that benefits from adaptive orchestration.
- Treat reasoning mode, agent topology, and authority topology as separate decisions.
- Allow parallel evidence, reasoning, and execution in either mode when ownership, acceptance, and recovery boundaries support them.
- Keep parallel writing available in Max and Ultra; use temporary single-writer recovery only after concrete repeated coupled failure, validation deadlock, or cyclic breakage.
- Preserve durable abstractions instead of hard-coding product fan-out defaults, fixed mode sequences, or numerical authority-risk formulas.

## Model Behavior Evidence

Model: `gpt-5.6-sol`

Reasoning efforts: `xhigh`, `max`, `ultra`

- Completed 30 isolated paired runs: five scenarios, three reasoning efforts, and two skill versions.
- The v0.6.1 candidate matched the accepted routing and topology in all 15 candidate runs.
- The v0.6.0 baseline matched 10 of 15 runs. All three stable known-causal runs unnecessarily staged Ultra before Max, and two of three shared-invariant pre-failure runs chose Ultra because useful parallel work was available even though one stable causal chain dominated.
- Both versions handled evolving decomposition, separable parallel implementation, and demonstrated failure-loop recovery consistently; the candidate retained those behaviors while making the Max/Ultra boundary more precise.
- Candidate runs allowed Max subagents, Ultra parallel writes, and bounded parallel evidence and reasoning without making coupling alone a single-writer trigger.
- Generated evaluation run logs were not retained in the repository.

## Release Boundary

This release changes the reusable skill, tests, and release evidence only. It does not migrate downstream projects, change installed skill links, alter model configuration or permissions, encode a product-specific default agent count, or perform publication actions.
